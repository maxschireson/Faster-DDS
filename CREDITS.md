# Credits and provenance

This work builds on [DDS](https://github.com/dds-bridge/dds), the open-source
double-dummy bridge solver created by Bo Haglund and developed by Soren Hein
and the DDS contributors. Their solver, search algorithms, data structures,
test infrastructure, and documentation are the foundation of these experiments.
All upstream copyright and Apache 2.0 license notices are retained.

The patches target upstream revision
`0f969b86a4841f438fd0cdff2924c4f2404abf6a`. They are research modifications,
not an official DDS release or a claim of authorship of the underlying solver.

Max Schireson initiated and directed this investigation, set the research goals,
and challenged the assumptions and interpretation of the results. OpenAI's
ChatGPT/Codex assisted with source review, implementation, experiment execution,
analysis, documentation, and preparation of the source upload.

The README distinguishes measured improvements from rejected prototypes and
speculative future work. No endorsement by the upstream authors is implied.
